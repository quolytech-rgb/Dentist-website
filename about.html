About SmileCare Dental

Learn about our mission, values, and the dedicated team behind your smile.

Our Story

Founded in 2010, SmileCare Dental has been serving our community with exceptional dental care for over a decade. We believe that everyone deserves a healthy, beautiful smile, and we're committed to making that vision a reality for each of our patients.

Our practice combines cutting-edge technology with a warm, personal approach to dentistry. We take the time to understand your unique needs and concerns, ensuring that every visit is comfortable and stress-free.

Meet Our Team

Dr. Sarah Johnson

Lead Dentist & Practice Owner

Dr. Johnson has over 15 years of experience in general and cosmetic dentistry. She graduated from Harvard School of Dental Medicine and is passionate about creating beautiful, healthy smiles.

Dr. Michael Chen

Orthodontist
